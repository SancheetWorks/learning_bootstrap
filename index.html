About Us

Welcome to Table Bite, your easy and convenient way to enjoy food at the food court. Simply scan the QR code at your table, explore the menu, place your order, and enjoy your meal without waiting in line. We make dining faster, easier, and more enjoyable.

Our goal is to connect customers, restaurants, and technology in one seamless platform. Table Bite brings multiple food outlets together, making it easier for customers to discover different cuisines and enjoy a convenient dining experience.
